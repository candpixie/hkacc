一

What this is

社論 · statement

Hong Kong produced one of the most influential film industries of the twentieth century, a literary tradition that barely reaches an American syllabus, a comics culture with its own grammar, and a pop songwriting practice built on a constraint almost no other language imposes. None of it is taught here. None of it has a room here.

NYU already runs the distinction we are asking for. The Ballet Club and the Ballet Collaborative both exist, because gathering around a thing and practising it are separate activities that need separate rooms.

The Hong Kong Students Association is the first kind and does it well. We are the second kind. Nobody is being replaced.

Coming attractions

預告

In person · workshop

Cantopop Lyrics Lab
Writing in Tone

Two hours on the problem in Exhibit 二. We listen to three songs and hear the constraint working, walk through how professional lyricists get around it, then split into groups to write and perform lyrics over a supplied melody.

Bilingual worksheets, so no Cantonese required. Native speakers and learners paired on purpose. A working Hong Kong lyricist joins for the last half hour to pull the results apart.

Online · recurring

Studio Visit
Hong Kong Artists at Work

An hour with a working Hong Kong artist, illustrator, or filmmaker, set in the New York morning so it lands in their evening. Work in progress, shown and explained.

The second half belongs to students, who bring their own work for criticism. Recorded with permission and posted, so the hour keeps working after it ends.

Admission

入會須知
  • 不需要 to be from Hong Kong
  • 不需要 a word of Cantonese
  • 不需要 to have seen any of the films
  • 不需要 dues, an audition, or an application

We expect most members will not be Hong Kong students. That is the design, not a concession. Everything runs bilingually and every session assumes somebody walked in cold. Open to every enrolled NYU student, every school.
