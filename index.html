一

What this is

手藝 craft, not company
聚

Gathering

The Hong Kong Students Association. People from home, in a room, eating. They do it well and we are not competing with it.

≠
做

Making

Films watched closely. Books read in both languages. Lyrics written, zines printed, translations argued over.

NYU already runs this distinction elsewhere. The Ballet Club and the Ballet Collaborative both exist. HKACC is the second kind.

二

What we do

  • 讀書會

    Reading and viewing circle

    Biweekly. One Hong Kong short story, poem, comic, or film sequence per session, read in Cantonese and English side by side, then taken apart together.

  • 電影season

    Hong Kong cinema series

    A themed screening series each semester with student or faculty introductions. New Wave, Wong Kar-wai, martial arts cinema as a craft tradition, post-handover independent film.

  • 講座

    Guest speakers

    Filmmakers, lyricists, illustrators, translators, and scholars. New York based in person, Hong Kong based over video in the New York morning so nobody has to be awake at 4am.

  • 工作坊

    Making workshops

    Zine and comic making, lettering and calligraphy, Cantonese lyric writing, subtitling and translation. Hands on the material, not just eyes on it.

  • 行街

    City outings

    Within the five boroughs. Museum of Chinese in America, Asia Society, Chinatown signage and architecture walks, Cantonese opera when it tours through.

  • 展覽

    Annual showcase

    One night a year where members show the Hong Kong and Cantonese inspired work they made, whether that is a film, a zine, a song, or a translation.

三

Two events, concretely

In person · workshop

Cantopop Lyrics Lab: Writing in Tone

Two hours on the central technical problem of Cantonese songwriting. We listen to three songs and hear the constraint working, walk through how professional lyricists get around it, then split into small groups to write and perform lyrics to a supplied melody.

Bilingual worksheets, so no Cantonese is required to take part. Native speakers and learners get paired on purpose. A working Hong Kong lyricist joins for the last half hour to pull the results apart.

Online · recurring

Studio Visit: Hong Kong Artists at Work

An hour with a working Hong Kong artist, illustrator, or filmmaker, scheduled in the New York morning so it lands in their evening. They show work in progress and walk through process.

The second half belongs to students, who bring their own work for feedback. Recorded with permission and posted, so the hour keeps working after it ends.

四

Who it is for

  • 不需要 to be from Hong Kong
  • 不需要 a word of Cantonese
  • 不需要 to have seen any of the films
  • 不需要 dues, an audition, or an application

We expect most members will not be Hong Kong students. That is the design, not a concession. Everything runs bilingually and every session assumes somebody walked in cold.

Hong Kong students get a room where their culture is studied rather than only celebrated. Everybody else gets a body of work that American syllabi skip almost entirely.

五

We are looking for founding members

HKACC is applying to the NYU Student Activities Board New Club Development Program this fall. We need a small founding group, and SAB requires that we are not all from one school, so students outside Tandon are especially wanted: Tisch, Steinhardt, CAS, Gallatin, Stern.

You do not need Hong Kong heritage or Cantonese. You need to care about the work and be willing to help build the first year of it.
