We study the work

We are a group of students who are enthusiastic about Hong Kong film, literature, Cantopop lyricism, comics, and the Cantonese language.

Since the 20th century, Hong Kong has produced one of the most influential film industries in the world, a literary tradition that rarely reaches an American curriculum, a comics culture such as 老夫子 with its own grammar, and a pop songwriting practice built on a constraint that is unparalleled.

HKACC is a club that does not just appreciate the work, but explores deeper into it. At each event we pick something unique about Hong Kong to learn about, bring in the people who made it, and then make things ourselves.

Everything runs bilingually, Cantonese and English side by side, so anyone can join in regardless of cultural background.

讀Read Hong Kong literature (香港文學), comics, and criticism, in Cantonese and English side by side.
睇Watch New Wave, post-handover independent film, martial arts cinema treated as a craft tradition.
做Make Lyrics, zines, translations, subtitles. Members have a chance to try composing as well.

啟事 · Notice

Screenings run with student or faculty introductions. Hong Kong guests appear by video in the New York morning, which is their evening. Workshops are bilingual and assume no Cantonese.

入會須知 Admission

  • No Hong Kong background required
  • No Cantonese required
  • No prior viewing or reading required
  • No dues, no audition, no application

Most members will not be Hong Kong students. That is the design, not a concession. Open to every enrolled NYU student, every school.

In person · workshop

Cantopop Lyrics Lab

Writing in Tone

Two hours on the problem in the plate above. Three songs, the constraint heard working, then groups write and perform lyrics over a supplied melody. Bilingual worksheets. Native speakers and learners paired on purpose. A working Hong Kong lyricist takes the last half hour to pull the results apart.

In person · workshop

Comic Drawing Workshop

Hong Kong comics, made by hand

A two hour workshop where you sit alongside likeminded artists and peers to brainstorm and draw, in a group or solo depending on your choice. Create something that enlightens you.

In person · screening

Screening Night

Hong Kong on a proper screen

One Hong Kong film, shown properly, with a short introduction from a student or a faculty member and a conversation afterwards. New Wave one month, post-handover independent film the next, martial arts cinema when we want to argue about choreography. Subtitles always on, so nobody is left out of the room.

In person · annual

Year-End Showcase

April, before finals swallow everyone

One night where members show what they have been making all year. A comic, a song, a translation, a zine, photographs from a trip back, notes from wherever you travelled. Work in progress counts, and so does something you started last week. The point is to put it in a room with people who will actually look at it.

The room opens
in spring.

HKACC has its founding four and goes to the Student Activities Board this month. SAB rules mean no club can run events before it is cleared, so the winter goes into building the reading list, locking in guest speakers, and growing this list. Events begin in the spring term with the Lyrics Lab and the first screening.
